Who is Neelam?

Student, Learner & Builder from Kaithal, Haryana

Growing up in Kaithal, Haryana, I was always curious about how things work behind a screen. Instead of just using websites and apps, I wanted to know how they are made. That curiosity pulled me towards coding, and I haven't looked back since.

Today I'm a Bachelor of Arts student at Kurukshetra University, and alongside my studies I'm building real skills. I completed my O Level course from NIELIT, and I'm now doing a Digital Marketing with AI course at NIELIT.

I work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I'm learning Python and IoT basics. I don't just watch tutorials, I build. A Telegram automation bot, a complete website for Tagore Global School, and games like Tic Tac Toe are all proof of that.

I enjoy creative work, I love working in a team, and I think quickly when problems come up. My goal is clear: to become a full stack developer who builds both the frontend and the backend. I'm not here just to learn, I'm here to build something I'm proud of.
